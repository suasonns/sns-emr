# Hospice Workflow Overview

## 1. Patient Admission

- Patient is referred to hospice
- RN completes admission assessment
- Physician or NP certifies hospice eligibility
- Admission date is established
- Medical Record Number (MRN) is assigned

Documentation:
- Admission note (RN)
- Certification of terminal illness (Physician/NP)
- Signed and dated by responsible clinician

---

## 2. Nursing Visits

- RN or LVN performs scheduled visits
- Vital signs, symptoms, and patient status are documented
- Education and interventions are recorded

Documentation:
- Visit note
- Date and time of visit
- Clinician signature upon finalization

---

## 3. Pain and Symptom Management

- Pain and symptoms assessed at each visit
- Medications adjusted as needed
- Changes communicated to physician or NP

Documentation:
- Symptom assessment
- Medication changes
- Orders signed by authorized provider

---

## 4. Plan of Care (POC)

- Interdisciplinary team establishes plan of care
- POC is reviewed regularly (IDG)
- Updates are documented and approved

Documentation:
- Plan of Care document
- Review dates
- Team member participation

---

## 5. Death or Discharge

- Death is pronounced and documented
- Family notified
- Discharge or death record completed

Documentation:
- Death note or discharge summary
- Date and time of death or discharge
- Signed by responsible clinician
