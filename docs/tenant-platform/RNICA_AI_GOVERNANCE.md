## 1. What RNICA AI Is

**[REPOSITORY-DISCOVERED]**, screenshot-confirmed (AI Action Center):
"Advisory Findings — For Clinician Review Only. These findings are
generated by the RNICA rules engine. They are strictly advisory and do
not replace clinical judgment."

- RNICA's current AI/Intelligence layer is a **rules engine**, not an
  LLM or generative model, for the advisory findings, missing-evidence,
  and compliance-signal content shown in the AI Action Center.
- The one confirmed exception is **structured findings extraction**
  (`backend/app/services/evidence/structured_findings.py`,
  `ai_extraction_service.py`), which reads documented narrative text to
  assert specific, registry-defined clinical concepts (see §3).

## 2. Refresh Trigger Rule (hard rule — do not violate)

**[REPOSITORY-DISCOVERED]**, screenshot-confirmed (AI Action Center
banner): "AI analysis last refreshed: [timestamp] (on last autosave).
Note: updates on Load, Save, and Lock."

- AI/Intelligence content refreshes **only** on: page Load, Save
  (autosave or manual), and Lock.
- **[DESIGN REQUIREMENT]** No screen may present AI output as
  continuously live or real-time. Every screen showing AI content must
  be able to show a last-refreshed timestamp or equivalent staleness
  indicator.
- A manual "Refresh AI Analysis" action is confirmed to exist
  (screenshot-confirmed, AI Action Center) and is the only user-
  initiated refresh path outside Load/Save/Lock.

## 3. What RNICA AI May Assert (Structured Findings)

**[REPOSITORY-DISCOVERED]** `structured_findings.py`'s `CONCEPT_REGISTRY`
(`~lines 140-170`) is the authoritative list of AI-assertable clinical
concepts. Confirmed exclusions/inclusions relevant to Functional Status:

- **Excluded** (not AI-assertable — computed scores, not transcribable
  facts): PPS, KPS, ECOG, FAST.
- **Included**: NYHA class (`PERF_NYHA_CLASS_I` through `IV`) — the sole
  performance scale the evidence-harvesting pipeline may assert from
  narrative text.
- **[DESIGN REQUIREMENT]** Any future concept added to `CONCEPT_REGISTRY`
  must go through the same registry mechanism — no ad hoc AI assertion
  paths outside this registry.

## 4. What RNICA AI May Never Do

**[REPOSITORY-DISCOVERED]**, screenshot-confirmed (AI Action Center
disclaimer): "AI does not generate narratives, definitive prognoses, or
physician certifications."

Restated as explicit prohibitions, consolidated from prior documents:

1. **No narrative generation.** AI may surface missing-narrative gaps
   (e.g., "Clinical Narrative not yet documented") but may never
   auto-write or auto-populate `finalization.clinicalNarrative` beyond a
   blank-only insertion point, per
   `RNICA_CLINICAL_NARRATIVE_FINAL_DECISION.md` (restated, not reopened).
2. **No definitive prognosis claims.** Findings are framed as advisory
   signals ("Documented Findings Requiring Review," "Compliance
   Signals"), never as a clinical determination.
3. **No physician certification.** LCD eligibility support is shown as
   an advisory summary ("LCD Supporting Evidence Present — 5 of 5
   criteria met... Review recommended"), never as a substitute for
   physician sign-off. Screenshot-confirmed: the LCD advisory panel says
   "Review recommended," not "certified" or "approved."
4. **No auto-created orders.** Suggested Orders on Screen 10 are
   advisory only; **[NOT BUILT / NOT CONNECTED]** no code path was found
   that lets a recommendation auto-create a physician order without an
   explicit user action, restated from `RNICA_WORKFLOW_AUTHORITY_MAP.md`.
5. **No mandated actions.** Every follow-up suggestion is explicitly
   labeled "SUGGESTION (NOT MANDATED)" — screenshot-confirmed (AI Action
   Center, Suggested Follow-Up panel, four items each carrying this
   label).
6. **No LLM claims.** No large-language-model text-generation capability
   is claimed or found for the advisory findings/compliance-signal
   content. Structured findings extraction (§3) is a separate, narrowly
   scoped concept-registry mechanism, not general-purpose generation.
7. **No claims of non-built capability.** The following are confirmed
   **[NOT BUILT / NOT CONNECTED]** and must never be described as active
   AI capability in any UI copy: CTI (automated Certification of
   Terminal Illness generation), F2F (automated Face-to-Face encounter
   analysis), Survey Readiness (automated CMS survey-readiness scoring),
   Billing Readiness (automated billing-readiness scoring), an
   Explanation Engine (automated rationale generation beyond the fixed
   advisory copy shown), a Patient Story Engine (automated narrative
   synthesis for Screen 1 beyond aggregation of existing evidence).

## 5. Consumer Boundaries

| Consumer | What it may read | What it may not do |
|---|---|---|
| Screen 1 (Patient Story) | RNICA Intelligence summary, Missing Information | May not originate new findings; presentation layer only, per `PATIENT_CHART_AUTHORITY_MAP.md` Patient Story Rule |
| Screen 12 (AI Action Center) | Full advisory findings, missing evidence, documentation gaps, compliance signals, suggested follow-up, LCD supporting evidence summary, readiness/compliance summary | May not gate Lock directly — Lock is gated by `RNICA_LOCK_READINESS_MATRIX.md`, which this screen only mirrors |
| Screen 5 (Diagnosis & LCD) | LCD eligibility advisory ("Advisory: LCD Eligibility Support") | May not write the LCD narrative field on the clinician's behalf — narrative remains clinician-authored |
| Screen 10 (Orders & POC) | Suggested Orders | May not create, submit, or approve an order without explicit clinician action |
| IDG (`IDGMeetingPatientReview`) | RNICA's existing AI output, as background context during review | **[NOT BUILT / NOT CONNECTED]** no confirmed dedicated AI engine for IDG itself — restated from `PATIENT_CHART_AUTHORITY_MAP.md` |

## 6. Chart-Wide AI Expansion — Explicitly Deferred

**[LOCKED PRODUCT DECISION]**, restated from `PATIENT_CHART_AUTHORITY_MAP.md`:
today's AI footprint is confined to Clinical Assessments (RNICA). This is
treated as an architectural advantage, not a gap. **[DESIGN REQUIREMENT]**
No implementation under this package may extend AI/Intelligence reads or
writes to any other patient-chart workspace (Visit Notes, Physician
Orders, Medications, IDG, CHHA, Communication Logs, Documents, Visit
Calendar, Bereavement, Volunteer Services) without a separately
authorized Intelligence Architecture document.

## 7. Implementation Checklist (per screen adding/using AI content)

Before implementing any AI-bearing UI element, confirm:

1. Is the content read-only and clearly labeled advisory? (§4.5)
2. Does it show a Load/Save/Lock-aligned refresh indicator? (§2)
3. Is its underlying concept in `CONCEPT_REGISTRY`, or is it rules-engine
   output rather than an asserted clinical fact? (§3)
4. Does it avoid narrative generation, prognosis claims, or
   certification language? (§4.1-4.3)
5. Does it avoid auto-creating orders, tasks, or mandated actions? (§4.4-4.5)
6. Does it stay within Clinical Assessments (RNICA) scope, not expand to
   another chart workspace? (§6)

If any answer is "no" or "unclear," raise it as a discovery/design
question before writing code — do not resolve it by assumption.
