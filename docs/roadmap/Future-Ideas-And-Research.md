# Future Ideas and Research

Status: Planning artifact — intake log. **Nothing here is implemented
directly.** Every future idea, from any source (user request,
observed workflow friction, analytics finding, brainstorm), goes here
first before being organized into a platform-specific roadmap
document.

## Required Fields Per Idea

Every idea logged in this document must contain:

- **Date Added**
- **Platform Area** (Owner / Tenant / Biller / AI Clinical
  Intelligence / Analytics & Agency Health / Audit Shield & Compliance
  / Cross-platform)
- **Problem Being Solved**
- **Expected Benefit**
- **Status**

## Status Values

- `Research`
- `Planned`
- `Future Consideration`
- `Approved Future Work`
- `Rejected`
- `Implemented`
