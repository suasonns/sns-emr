# Analytics and Agency Health Roadmap

Status: Planning artifact — future-development repository.
**Do not implement items from this document unless explicitly instructed.**

## Purpose

Analytics are NOT for patient review.
Analytics are NOT for clinical review.
Analytics exist to identify operational friction and future product
opportunities.

The purpose is:

- Determine what agencies struggle with.
- Determine what SNS should improve next.
- Use actual platform usage to prioritize roadmap decisions.

## Track and Trend

- Alert Frequency
- Alert Categories
- Alert Trends
- Agency Health
- Workflow Friction
- Most Common Corrections
- Most Common Failures
- Most Common Guidance Requests
- Most Common Finalization Warnings
- Most Common Reopened Workflows
- Most Common Documentation Delays
- Most Common Assistance Requests

## Relationship to Other Roadmaps

This is the measurement layer that feeds prioritization decisions
back into the Owner Platform Roadmap (agency health, adoption
monitoring) and the Tenant Platform Roadmap (workflow coaching,
documentation burden reduction). It does not itself define new
features — it defines what to measure so future feature decisions
are evidence-based rather than anecdotal.
