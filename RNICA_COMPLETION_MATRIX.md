- HOPE Symptom Impact section (symptomImpact.*) is not directly wired, even though transcript AI severity extraction exists and currently writes related body-system fields instead.

- Objective GI/GU device-detail fields (feeding tube site, ostomy condition, catheter size/dates/irrigation/care) have no concept/apply path.
- Endocrine insulin-detail fields (insulin type/dose, HbA1c date, symptomSeverity map) are not wired despite adjacent endocrine concepts existing.
- Cardiovascular edema pitting and respiratory ventilator settings remain unmapped structured fields.
